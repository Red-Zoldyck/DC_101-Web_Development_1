WHAT IS TRAIL RATED®

A Trail Rated® badge is not given. It’s earned. Every Trail Rated 4x4 Jeep® Brand vehicle has succeeded against a series of grueling tests in five categories: Traction, Water Fording, Maneuverability, Articulation, and Ground Clearance.

TRACTION

Jeep® Brand Trail Rated® 4x4 vehicles have the ability to handle some of the harshest, most unpredictable driving conditions. Whether you’re looking to take on tough terrain or make it home through a snowstorm, your Jeep® can conquer it.

WATER FORDING

With Trail Rated® capability on your side, you can traverse bodies of water that other vehicles wouldn’t dare attempt. Electrical connections and body openings are sealed, and the air intake is positioned higher to protect your vehicle when driving through overflowing creeks, streams, and deep puddles.

MANEU-VERABILITY

Swiftly dodge obstacles, veer around narrow gaps, or safely navigate emergency situations. Thanks to precision steering and optimized wheelbases, your Jeep® can handle tight trail switchbacks and emergency scenarios.

ARTICULATION

Good articulation helps you tackle rough terrain, such as areas with construction or deep potholes. It ensures your wheels remain stable when one or more tires are off the ground, providing maximum suspension flexibility.

GROUND CLEARANCE

Trail Rated® gives you plenty of room between the underside of your vehicle and the terrain, allowing you to drive over rocks and logs without damage. Higher levels of ground clearance provide more options for tackling obstacles when going around isn’t possible.

WHERE WE TEST

RUBICON TRAIL, CALIFORNIA

Trail Rated® testing begins in state-of-the-art facilities at FCA US headquarters before heading out to some of the most challenging terrain on earth. Jeep® Brand vehicles conquer harsh conditions like the unforgiving boulders of the Rubicon.

MOAB, UTAH

Jeep® Brand Trail Rated 4x4 vehicles don’t just endure; they conquer the challenging terrain and weather conditions that would break lesser vehicles. The toughest tests push Jeep vehicles to their limits and beyond.

WHY WE TEST

Pushing our vehicles as far as they can go is part of the Jeep® Brand DNA. As long as we strive for groundbreaking capability and you pursue exhilarating adventure, Jeep® Brand Trail Rated® vehicles will continue to be tested to handle everything you encounter.
